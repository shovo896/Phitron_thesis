\section*{Purpose}
This file gives a Bangladesh-marketplace-style procurement budget for the A$^2$SNN microcontroller experiment. Prices are written in BDT and should be treated as working estimates because local electronics prices change with stock, import source, delivery charge, and seller warranty.

\section*{Budget Assumptions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Target platform: ESP32-S3 class board, because it is locally available and has enough flash/PSRAM variants for TinyML/SNN prototyping.
    \item Measurement goal: report latency, cycles, flash, SRAM, and approximate board-level power/energy.
    \item Buying mode: Bangladesh online electronics shops and marketplaces such as TechShopBD, RoboticsBD, ElectroXBD, BDTronics, Daraz, or equivalent local sellers.
    \item Delivery charge: estimated separately because it depends on city and seller.
    \item Final thesis/paper values must use invoice prices and measured hardware data.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Minimum Experiments}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Train compact ANN baseline.
    \item Train or convert fixed-duration SNN baseline.
    \item Quantize SNN weights to INT8.
    \item Implement dense MCU inference loop.
    \item Implement sparse event-driven MCU inference loop.
    \item Add confidence-aware early exit.
    \item Measure latency, cycles, flash, SRAM, power, and energy.
    \item Repeat for at least three random seeds if training variance is significant.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Measurement Notes}
For latency, wrap inference with a GPIO toggle and confirm timing with a logic analyzer when possible. For power, record supply voltage, current measurement method, sampling rate, and whether USB serial, Wi-Fi, camera, and LEDs are enabled. Do not present USB-tester readings as chip-only energy; label them as board-level energy.
